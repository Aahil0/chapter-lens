    How to read the score

    Flesch Reading Ease estimates difficulty from sentence length and syllables per word. Higher scores suggest easier reading: 90+ very easy, 80–89 easy, 70–79 fairly easy, 60–69 standard, 50–59 fairly difficult, 30–49 difficult, below 30 very difficult. Scores can fall outside 0–100.

    We rank sentences using the same formula and highlight the five lowest scores (or all sentences if there are fewer than five). Syllable counting and sentence boundaries are estimates, particularly for names, abbreviations and unusual formatting. This English-only tool measures reading complexity, not writing quality. Fiction may deliberately use complex prose.
